# pandoc

Document conversion with Pandoc: Markdown, Word, HTML, LaTeX, EPUB, PDF and slides, citations, batch conversion and Arabic/RTL PDFs. Asks before overwriting outputs, uses --sandbox for untrusted documents, and installs Pandoc/LaTeX only with your consent.
