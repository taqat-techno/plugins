# remotion

Narrated Remotion videos whose voice never cuts between scenes: project init with continuous-audio templates, edge-tts narration (text is sent to Microsoft's online TTS), MP3 concatenation with frame-exact scene timestamps, and render presets. Remotion itself needs a company licence for larger for-profit organisations.
